Privacy Policy

At PitchDetectors.com, accessible from https://pitchdetectors.com, we focus on protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when using audiobook calculators and how that information is handled.

What Information Is Collected

We do not require users to submit personal data to use audiobook calculators. Inputs such as audiobook duration, playback speed, or listening time are processed in real time and are not stored. Basic technical data like browser type, device type, and IP address may be collected automatically for analytics and security.

How Calculator Data Is Used

All calculator inputs are used only to generate immediate results based on standard time and speed formulas. This data is not saved, shared, or linked to individual users. Outputs depend entirely on the values entered during the session.

Cookies and Advertising

This website may use cookies to improve functionality and user experience. Third-party services such as Google AdSense may use cookies to display relevant ads based on browsing behavior. Users can control or disable cookies through browser settings.

How Information Is Used

  • To maintain and improve website performance
  • To analyze usage patterns for optimization
  • To display relevant advertisements through third-party services

Third-Party Services

Third-party providers, including advertising networks, may collect and process data under their own policies. We do not control their data practices. For example, Google’s privacy policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

Data Security

Reasonable measures are implemented to protect data from unauthorized access. However, no internet-based system guarantees complete security, and transmission risks may exist.

Children’s Privacy

This website does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If such data is identified, it will be removed upon request.

User Consent

By using PitchDetectors.com, you agree to this Privacy Policy and its terms.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The most recent version will always be published on this page.
