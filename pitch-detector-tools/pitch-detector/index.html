How it works

Our free audio pitch analyzer gives you instant feedback by interpreting your microphone's input in real time.

  • 1.Click Start and allow microphone access.
  • 2.Sing or play a steady note; detection happens in real time.
  • 3.Read the note, exact frequency (Hz), and the ±cents meter.

Remember to use a quiet, echo-free room and hold a single, steady note for several seconds to get the most accurate and stable reading from the tool.

Accuracy & methodology

Our tool prioritizes stable, accurate readings. We use an autocorrelation algorithm to find the fundamental frequency, a method robust against overtones. The system intelligently handles different device sample rates for consistent results everywhere. A built-in noise gate automatically ignores quiet background sounds, so only your intended note is analyzed.

Best of all, every calculation happens directly on your device. This local, on-device processing means your audio is never uploaded, guaranteeing your privacy while providing instant, low-latency feedback.
