Formulas & Acoustic Notes
  • Room Volume: V = L × W × H (Length × Width × Height)
  • Cubic Meters Conversion: V(m³) = V(ft³) × 0.0283168
  • Subwoofer Recommendation: 1 subwoofer per ~1500 ft³ for balanced low-frequency coverage
  • Consider room modes, standing waves, absorption, SPL efficiency when placing subwoofers for even bass distribution
  • High ceilings or irregular shapes may require multiple subwoofers or acoustic treatment (bass traps, diffusers)
  • Use correct speaker placement, orientation, and boundary effects to avoid nulls and peaks
