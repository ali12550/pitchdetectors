Who We Are

Pitch Detectors is a project built for audio enthusiasts, creators, and everyday listeners who want smart, easy-to-use tools for their media needs. From BPM converters to playback speed calculators and audiobook percentage tools, we make sound math simple and accessible.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make complex audio and time-based calculations intuitive and accessible. Whether you’re optimizing podcast playback, calculating file size, or planning music production sessions, our tools provide instant answers with accuracy and simplicity.

💡 What Makes Us Different

We focus on precision, clean design, and an ad-free user experience. Every calculator on Pitch Detectors is tested, refined, and built with creators in mind.

Our Story

Launched in 2024, Pitch Detectors started as a small idea to simplify playback speed calculations. Today, it has grown into a community-driven platform offering smart tools, guides, and insights for everyone passionate about music, sound, and audio creation.

Future Plans

  • 🎧 Add more AI-powered audio calculators for creators and musicians
  • 📗 Launch a knowledge base for audio learners and enthusiasts
  • 🌍 Support global creators and students with free, accessible utilities
