Mixing Recommendations

    Camelot Wheel Guide

    The Camelot Wheel organizes musical keys for easy harmonic mixing:

    • Same key: Perfect compatibility
    • ±1 number: Adjacent keys mix well (8A → 7A or 9A)
    • Same number, different letter: Relative major/minor (8A ↔ 8B)
    • Energy boost: +1 on wheel increases energy
    • Energy drop: -1 on wheel decreases energy
