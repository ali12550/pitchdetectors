Formula Used

Accuracy is maximized when duration, playback speed, and word count are entered correctly. This audiobook speed calculator ⏳ calculates:

  • Adjusted Listening Time = Original Duration ÷ Playback Speed
  • Time Saved = Original Duration − Adjusted Listening Time
  • Estimated Completion = Current Progress ÷ Playback Speed

For word-count-based calculations, the audiobook reading speed calculator formula is:

  • Estimated Duration = Word Count ÷ Words per Minute (WPM)
