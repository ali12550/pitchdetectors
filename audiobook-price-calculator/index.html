Audiobook Price Calculator — Estimate Production & Cost Efficiently

The Audiobook Price Calculator estimates the total cost to produce an audiobook based on key cost drivers like narrator rate per finished hour, audiobook length (hours/minutes), and optional production fees. This tool acts as an audiobook cost estimator, helping authors, narrators, and publishers budget audiobook creation accurately.

How the Calculator Works

This tool calculates total audiobook production cost using three core inputs:

  1. Audiobook Length (hours/minutes) — Total listening time to produce.
  2. Narrator Rate per Finished Hour (PFH) — Standard industry cost for narrator work.
  3. Optional Production Fees — Editing, mastering, or distribution costs.

Core formula used:

  • Total Cost = (Audiobook Length × Narrator Rate per Finished Hour) + Production Fees

The result shows the estimated total cost to produce an audiobook in your preferred currency.

Why Use an Audiobook Price Calculator

  • Budget audiobook production accurately by estimating cost per finished hour.
  • Compare narrator rates and see how they affect total cost.
  • Plan for editing or mastering fees as part of the production budget.
  • Make informed publishing decisions before recording starts.

This tool is ideal for indie authors, small publishers, narrators, or anyone planning an audiobook project.

Inputs You Need

Audiobook Length

The total duration of the finished audiobook (hours and minutes). Longer audiobooks cost more to produce.If you have a manuscript but have not recorded yet, use the Audiobook Length Calculator to estimate finished hours from word count or page count before entering a value here.

Narrator Rate per Finished Hour

Narration costs are usually billed per finished hour (PFH). Lower rates reduce overall cost; higher rates reflect experienced narrators.

Production Fees (Optional)

Include editing, mastering, proofing, or distribution fees to refine the total cost estimate.

What This Calculator Does NOT Calculate

To maintain precise intent, this calculator does not estimate:

  • Audiobook listening duration or time remaining
  • Income or royalty splits
  • Retail price or revenue distributions
  • Per-page or word-count time estimates

To estimate audiobook duration from a manuscript, use the Audiobook Length Calculator. To track listening progress or completion percentage, use the Audiobook Progress Calculator.

Audiobook Pricing Terms You Should Know

  • Finished Hour (PFH): The time of completed audiobook content, used for narrator billing.Note: finished hour refers to the final recorded duration at normal playback speed. To see how that duration changes for listeners at 1.25x or 1.5x, use the Audiobook Speed Calculator.
  • Narrator Rate: Amount paid per finished hour for voice work.
  • Production Fees: Costs associated with editing, mastering, proofing, or other production tasks.
  • Total Audiobook Cost: Combined cost of narration and production fees.
