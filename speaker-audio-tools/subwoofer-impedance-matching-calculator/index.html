How to Use the Subwoofer Impedance Matching Calculator

Use this tool to optimize subwoofer wiring for your system. Whether wiring series vs parallel subwoofers or combining them in a series-parallel configuration, this calculator ensures proper impedance matching with your amplifier to avoid clipping and overloading the amplifier load. Proper setup improves subwoofer efficiency and overall dynamic range.

  1. Enter the total number of subwoofers you want in your subwoofer bank.
  2. Provide each subwoofer’s impedance (Ω) to calculate total load.
  3. Select your wiring configuration: Series, Parallel, or Series-Parallel.
  4. If using series-parallel, indicate how many subs per series group to optimize home theater subwoofer design or car audio optimization.
  5. The calculator shows the total impedance, helping with safe PA system tuning and minimizing voltage drop.
Formulas Used:
  • Series: Ztotal = Z1 + Z2 + ... + Zn
  • Parallel: 1/Ztotal = 1/Z1 + 1/Z2 + ... + 1/Zn
  • Series-Parallel: Total = sum of series group impedances in parallel

FAQ

Proper impedance matching ensures your amplifier runs efficiently without overheating or clipping, protecting both the amp and subwoofers.

Incorrect wiring can create a total load outside the amplifier’s safe range, causing clipping, distortion, or potential damage to the amplifier.

Mixing subwoofers with different impedances complicates total load calculations and is not recommended unless you carefully compute the series-parallel configuration.
