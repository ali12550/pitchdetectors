Audible Credits Calculator

An Audible credits calculator answers the question every subscriber eventually asks: is Audible actually worth it for me? A credit feels like a free book, but you are paying for it through your membership — and the real price depends on your plan, how many credits you actually use, and what the books you pick would cost at retail.

Enter your plan, the credits you use per year, and the typical retail price of the audiobooks you buy. The calculator shows your effective cost per credit, your total yearly spend, and exactly how much you save (or lose) compared with buying those same books outright.

What Is an Audible Credits Calculator?

Audible's Premium Plus plans give you credits — usually one per month — and each credit buys any Premium audiobook regardless of its list price. That sounds like a great deal until you do the math: a $14.95 monthly plan costs $179.40 a year, so each of your 12 credits effectively costs $14.95. If the books you redeem typically sell for $25–$30, you come out ahead. If you mostly pick $10 sale titles, you might be overpaying.

This calculator makes that comparison concrete. It also handles the cheaper Audible Plus plan, which has no credits at all — just unlimited access to the Plus catalog — so you can see which tier honestly fits your listening habits.

How to Use the Audible Credits Calculator

  1. Choose your Audible plan — Premium Plus Monthly, Premium Plus Annual, or Audible Plus.
  2. Enter the credits you actually use per year. Be honest: unused credits that pile up still cost you money.
  3. Enter the average retail price of the audiobooks you redeem — check a few recent picks in the Audible store.
  4. Click Calculate to see your cost per credit, yearly total, retail value, and savings.
  5. Read the verdict. If savings are strongly positive, your plan is earning its keep. If not, consider switching plans or pausing.

The Formula Behind the Calculator

The calculation has four simple steps:

  • Yearly cost = Monthly price × 12 (or the flat annual price for the yearly plan)
  • Effective cost per credit = Yearly cost ÷ Credits used per year
  • Retail value = Credits used × Average retail price
  • Savings = Retail value − Yearly cost

A positive savings number means the membership beats buying outright. A negative number means you would spend less just purchasing the books — or that the cheaper Plus catalog plan suits you better.

Tips for Getting the Most From Audible Credits

  • Spend credits on expensive books. The golden rule: redeem credits for titles over $20 and buy cheap ones outright during sales.
  • Use every credit. An unused credit is money already spent. Set a monthly reminder to redeem before new credits arrive.
  • Consider the annual plan if you are committed. It drops the per-credit cost from $14.95 to about $12.46 with the same 12 credits.
  • Try Plus first if you listen a lot but buy little. At $7.95/month with unlimited catalog listening, heavy listeners often do better without credits.
  • Pause instead of canceling. Audible lets you pause for up to 3 months — you keep your library and credits while skipping payments during slow periods.
  • Watch the sales. During 2-for-1 and $4.95 sales, buying outright can beat credit value — stack the calculator against the sale price.
