What Is BPM to Milliseconds?

BPM to milliseconds conversion calculates how long each beat lasts in milliseconds based on a track's tempo. Formula: Milliseconds per Beat = 60,000 ÷ BPM. For example, at 120 BPM, each beat lasts 500 milliseconds. This is vital for drum programming, MIDI sequencing, and live DJing.

What Is Milliseconds to BPM?

The inverse calculation converts the duration of a beat in milliseconds into BPM. Formula: BPM = 60,000 ÷ Milliseconds per Beat. For example, if a beat lasts 400 ms, the BPM is 150. This helps producers sync tracks or samples accurately.
