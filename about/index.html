Who We Are

PitchDetectors.com is a utility platform focused on audiobook calculators. It provides tools that help users calculate listening time, adjust playback speed, estimate completion dates, and track audiobook progress. The platform is designed for listeners who want clear, data-based insights into their audiobook consumption without manual calculations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify audiobook listening calculations using accurate, input-based tools. By turning duration, speed, and session data into measurable outputs, we help users plan their listening schedules, improve time management, and understand how playback settings affect total listening time.

💡 What Makes These Tools Useful

Each calculator is built on standard time and speed formulas. Users can input audiobook length, playback speed, or daily listening duration to get instant results. Outputs are consistent, easy to interpret, and require no technical knowledge.

Our Story

PitchDetectors.com started as a focused project to solve a specific problem: calculating audiobook listening time accurately. Many listeners rely on playback speed adjustments, but lack clear tools to measure the impact. This platform was created to provide simple calculators that remove guesswork and deliver precise estimates.

Future Plans

  • 🎧 Expand audiobook-related calculators for deeper listening insights
  • 📊 Add advanced tools for tracking and comparing listening habits
  • 🌍 Maintain free and accessible utilities for global audiobook users
