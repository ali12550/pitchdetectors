Audiobook Tools

Free audiobook calculators for listeners, authors, and narrators. Estimate listening time, track progress, calculate audiobook length, and plan production costs.

Audiobook Speed Calculator

Enter audiobook duration and playback speed to see adjusted listening time and total time saved at 1.25x, 1.5x, or 2x.

Audiobook Length Calculator

Enter word count or page count to estimate total audiobook duration in hours and minutes. Useful for authors and narrators in pre-production.

Audiobook Progress Calculator

Enter total duration and time listened to see completion percentage, time remaining, and estimated finish date at any playback speed.

Audiobook Percentage Calculator

Enter total duration and time listened to calculate your exact audiobook completion percentage as a single numeric value.

Audiobook Price Calculator

Enter audiobook length and narrator rate per finished hour to estimate total production cost. Add editing and mastering fees for a full budget breakdown.

Audiobook Goal Calculator

Planning a reading challenge? Enter your yearly book goal and see exactly how many minutes per day you need to listen.

Audible Credits Calculator

Compare Audible plans and credit costs against retail book prices to see if your subscription is really saving you money.

Audiobook Sleep Timer Calculator

Fall asleep to audiobooks? Calculate the ideal sleep-timer length and see where you'll be in the book when it stops.

Audiobook vs Reading Time Calculator

Compare print reading time against audiobook listening time at any playback speed — see which format is faster for you.

Which audiobook tool do you need?

Each tool in this suite serves a specific calculation. Use this guide to find the right one for your situation.

  • If you want to know how long an audiobook will take at faster speeds — use the Audiobook Speed Calculator.
  • If you have a manuscript and need to estimate recording length — use the Audiobook Length Calculator.
  • If you want to track how far into an audiobook you are — use the Audiobook Progress Calculator.
  • If your app does not show completion percentage — use the Audiobook Percentage Calculator.
  • If you are planning production costs before recording — use the Audiobook Price Calculator.
  • If you're doing a yearly reading challenge — use the Audiobook Goal Calculator to find your required daily listening minutes.
  • If you wonder whether Audible is worth it — use the Audible Credits Calculator to compare plan costs vs retail prices.
  • If you fall asleep listening — use the Audiobook Sleep Timer Calculator to set the perfect timer.
  • If you can't decide between reading and listening — use the Audiobook vs Reading Time Calculator to compare both formats.

Tools for every kind of audiobook user

For listeners

Most listeners come here with one of three questions: how long will this take?, how far along am I?, and am I on pace? The length calculator answers the first before you even press play. The progress calculator and percentage calculator answer the second mid-book. And the goal calculator answers the third across your whole reading year. Add the speed calculator when you want to squeeze more books into the same commute, and the sleep timer calculator for bedtime listening sessions.

For authors and narrators

If you create audiobooks, start with the length calculator to turn your manuscript's word count into finished hours, then feed that number into the price calculator to budget narrator fees (per finished hour) plus editing and mastering. Our production budget guide walks through the full economics, from PFH rates to break-even math.

How the tools work together

The calculators are designed as a chain: estimate length → adjust for speed → track progress → hit your goal. For example, use the length calculator to find that a novel is 11 hours, the speed calculator to see it drops to 7h 20m at 1.5x, the progress calculator to track where you are each evening, and the goal calculator to confirm you're on pace for 40 books this year. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — no account, no uploads, and your inputs never leave your device.

Frequently asked questions

Are these audiobook calculators free?

Yes — every tool on this page is free, works in any modern browser, and requires no signup or download.

Do the calculators work with Audible, Spotify, and Apple Books?

Yes. The math is platform-independent: enter the duration shown in any app and the results are the same.

Is my data saved or uploaded anywhere?

No. All calculations happen locally in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing you type is sent to our servers.

Which tool should I start with?

If you're a listener, start with the progress calculator. If you're an author or narrator, start with the length calculator.
