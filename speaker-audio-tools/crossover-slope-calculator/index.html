Crossover Slope Calculator – Audio Design Tool

Overview

This Crossover Slope Calculator helps you compute speaker crossover filter slopes, including high‑pass and low‑pass roll‑offs, based on your desired frequency and filter order. Designed for audio technicians and DIY speaker builders, this free online tool works right in your browser without downloads.

Who Should Use This Tool

  • DIY speaker builders
  • Audio engineers
  • Home audio enthusiasts
  • Car audio system designers
  • Professional installers working with passive crossover networks

Core Concepts

  • Passive crossover network design
  • High-pass and low-pass filters
  • Speaker impedance (Ω)
  • Roll-off slope (dB/oct)
  • Phase alignment and summing

Formulas Used

Capacitor (C)

C = 1 / (2π × f × R)

Inductor (L)

L = R / (2π × f)
